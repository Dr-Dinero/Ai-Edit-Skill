# Director brain

Distilled from the social media director's 16-part knowledge base (Keegan, reconstructed
27 October 2026). The full private originals live in the project's shared files, not in
this public repo. Every council role reads the universal gate and its own section before
a job. When a new correction from Keegan or the owner arrives, add it under "Documented
corrections" with the date, and update the affected role section.

## How to weigh this file

- **Direct correction**: something Keegan said about a real asset. Strongest evidence.
- **Default**: a historical habit (for example 3-5 hashtags). Use when it fits; never
  present it as a platform law.
- **Proposed**: a workflow or score built for the AI. A tool, not Keegan's private rule.
- **Unknown**: the record does not say. Ask, or state the assumption, but never invent
  his opinion, a metric, a threshold, keyword volume, or a result.

This file is reference and feedback, not the rulebook. For SEO, titles, captions,
hashtags, and posting, `../packager/references/house-rules.md` decides (owner,
2026-10-28). Precedence: the owner's instruction for this job, then the house rules,
then this file. Use Keegan's corrections to catch mistakes he has already flagged
(spelling, branding, event status, likeness, duplicate CTAs, tone) and to inform taste.

## Universal gate (every role, every asset)

1. Is the real source (footage, transcript, photo, brand reference) in hand? If not,
   request it or label the work provisional.
2. Is every claim supported by the source? If not, revise or hold.
3. Is it on brand and factually exact: names, spelling, scripture, event, date, status?
4. Does it work where it will actually live: phone crop, profile-photo overlap, safe
   zones, playback, audio, existing overlays?
5. Does the energy match the content? Intensity fits context: a calm studio session
   gets calm copy; a revival gets energy.
6. Is it approved by the right person? Silence is never approval. Nothing publishes,
   sends, or spends without authorization.

Any failure blocks "ready", whatever the score.

## Role sections

### virality-director (brief)
- Rank real, timestamped moments from the actual footage; never infer the best moment
  from a topic alone. Record source/timecode, audience, opening, payoff, platform,
  packaging, next step, and risk or permissions for each idea.
- Write three distinct hooks: a direct question, a real tension, a specific outcome the
  footage proves.
- Proposed score, 0-2 each: evidence fit, audience relevance, opening clarity, payoff,
  brand, feasible production. A factual, rights, or dignity failure rejects the idea
  regardless of score.
- Name the content's energy in the brief (calm, teaching, encounter, event, promo) so
  every later role matches it.
- Encounters: preserve dignity, consent and outcome context. Escalate to the owner for
  minors, distressed participants, unclear permission, or doctrinal disputes.

### council-editor (edit)
- Keep the setup and payoff that make the story true; cut only what is redundant.
- Before adding a caption, banner, or CTA, check what is already burned in. One CTA.
- A requested re-edit must look materially different from the original.
- Music, B-roll, zooms and effects only when they serve the content and are licensed.
  Keegan's taste for zoom cadence, transitions, music and caption style is unknown;
  follow `docs/EDITING-PLAYBOOK.md` and the brief, and log choices so he can react.

### edit-qc
- Play the export on a phone-sized frame with sound: plays, audio present and synced,
  correct orientation, nothing cropped, captions accurate, no duplicated CTA, banner
  text spelled right.
- Compare against the source: is meaning preserved, and is a requested remake
  substantive?
- Fail with the exact defect, timestamp, and owner.

### packager (titles, SEO, thumbnails, captions)
- Titles: three distinct angles (topic-forward, curiosity grounded in content,
  outcome-led only if shown). Include required event branding such as "TSNL Legacy
  Conference panel" without losing clarity. Recommend one and say the tradeoff.
- Description: follow the house-rules structure; keep it tight, with scripture only
  if it truly connects and one CTA. Verify every name, verse, date, and link.
- Keywords: natural viewer phrases. Without live research, call them hypotheses; never
  cite search volumes you did not measure.
- Captions: hashtag and tag counts follow the house rules. Keegan's notes show a
  habit of first person in Apostle Daniel's voice; use it only when the account and
  voice fit, otherwise TSNL third person.
- Thumbnails: real, sharp photos from the job (never AI-repainted faces); hierarchy per
  the brief; exact brand system for the event; text readable at phone size; "Upcoming"
  if the destination is not live; check mobile crop and safe zones. Bright and vivid
  for livestreams; calm when the content is calm.
- Each platform gets its own copy; Twitch and Kick describe the actual live activity.

### packaging-qc
- Fail on: unsupported claim, misspelling, wrong event branding or status, AI-looking
  or blurry likeness, unreadable phone text, duplicated CTA, dead or unavailable
  destination, copy pasted across platforms.
- Proposed readiness score, 0-5 each: message accuracy, audience relevance, hook and
  title, branding and likeness, delivery and playback, platform fit, CTA and function,
  story and payoff, voice, execution readiness (out of 50). 40+ ready for owner review,
  30-39 revise, under 30 rebuild. Unvalidated working bands; hard gates override.

### analyst
- State the goal, window, baseline, sample size, and metric definitions before any
  claim. Compare like with like (format, length, platform, age).
- Diagnose with competing hypotheses, not causes: low impressions (topic or audience
  fit), high impressions with low CTR (packaging), strong CTR with weak early retention
  (opening breaks the promise), strong views with low action (CTA or destination).
- Propose one controlled test with a decision rule. Historical schedules (long-form
  Tue 5 pm and Sat 11 am, vlogs Tue/Thu 5 pm, 3-6 Shorts per channel weekly) are
  history, not current policy; confirm before using.
- Never quote unverified revenue, download, or vendor view claims as proof.

### community (DM and comment replies, when built)
- Identify who is speaking and to whom; match the account's voice and the sender's
  tone. First person as Apostle only when authorized.
- Never invent testimony, promise healing or outcomes, create false urgency, or give
  event, price, or link details that are not verified live.
- Escalate prayer requests, crises, minors, money, health, legal, doctrinal, or
  sensitive personal stories to a human. Drafts wait for approval by default.

## Learning loop

For each piece of feedback record: the asset, Keegan's or the owner's exact words, the
date, the decision, the reusable rule, confidence, and any counterexample. One approval
covers that asset, not a permanent law. Ask before overturning an existing rule.
